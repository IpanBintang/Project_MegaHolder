Burger TopHat®

We're putting the finishing touches on our story.
Come back soon to learn about our flame-grilled heritage, our people, and what makes every bite worth it.

Our Flame-Grilled Heritage

Burger TopHat® has been serving flame-grilled goodness since our inception. Our commitment to quality, fresh ingredients, and exceptional service has made us a beloved choice for burger lovers everywhere.

Every burger is crafted with care, featuring premium beef grilled to perfection on our signature flame grill. We believe that great burgers start with great ingredients and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Experience Burger TopHat®

Our Journey

1999 🎩

The TopHat Legacy Begins

Burger TopHat® was born with a simple vision: to serve flame-grilled burgers that celebrate quality and taste. Our first restaurant opens its doors in Kedah, Malaysia.

2006 🍔

Expansion Across Malaysia

With our commitment to excellence, Burger TopHat® expands to multiple locations. We introduce our signature flame-grilled technique that becomes legendary among burger lovers.

2018 ⭐

Digital Revolution

Burger TopHat® launches its digital presence with online ordering and delivery services. We embrace innovation while maintaining our core commitment to quality.

2020 🤝

Community First Initiative

During challenging times, we stay committed to serving our communities. Burger TopHat® launches special programs to support local suppliers and our team members.

2026 🚀

The Future is Now

Celebrating our heritage while innovating for tomorrow. Burger TopHat® continues to redefine the burger experience with sustainable practices and unforgettable moments.
