Our Story

Killer Gourmet Burgers — your casual dining space bursting with raw energy and delicious handcrafted burgers, fries & desserts. Indulge in our chin-dripping juicy burgers, made with serious passion for the passionate.

After all, life is too short for a bad burger.

The KGB Experience

Real Food, Real People

We believe the best burgers can only be made with the best ingredients. Our fluffy brioche buns are baked daily. Beef, chicken, and vegetarian patties are freshly prepared daily. KGB’s burger cooks are masters of the art.

Casual Dining

We remove the fancy parts of gourmet burger dining and maintain a casual setting with down-to-earth prices — so your comfort food cravings can be satisfied without breaking the bank.

Beef Doneness Levels

Our beef patties are ground in-house daily from 100% prime cuts of Aussie Angus beef. Burger connoisseurs can safely enjoy medium doneness (or below).

Burger Styles

We serve beef burgers ultra smashed ‘diner style’ or flame grilled ‘gourmet style’. Chicken burgers are grilled or deep-fried till golden. Non-meat eaters can enjoy grilled vegetarian patties and mushroom.

Sidekicks

4 choices of regular fries, 4 choices of specialty handcrafted fries, chicken wings, milkshakes and more to complete your gourmet burger experience.

Toppings

From classic caramelized onions and sharp cheddar to espresso bacon jam with maple-glazed cornflakes — our toppings are anything but ordinary.
