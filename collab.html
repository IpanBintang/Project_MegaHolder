The Story Behind It

Two Worlds.
One Unholy Union.

Burger TopHat® has always been about bold flavours and daring combinations. MYSTIKS Horror Journalism has spent years documenting the darkest corners of storytelling, cinema, and culture. This collab was inevitable.

For a limited time, we're merging the warmth of flame-grilled burgers with the chill of horror's finest editorial voice — producing a menu, a series of collector packaging, and exclusive events that blur the line between feast and fright.

Every item in the Dark Collab Menu is inspired by a MYSTIKS horror archetype: the Vampire, the Wraith, and the Revenant. Taste the story.

From MYSTIKS Editors

Why We Said Yes to Burgers

Horror has always been about confronting the things we fear — and sometimes, the most terrifying thing is a blank stomach at 2am. When BTH approached us, we saw an opportunity to merge two visceral experiences.

The best horror unsettles you. A great burger does the same. This collab was a no-brainer for us — and we designed every packaging detail ourselves.
