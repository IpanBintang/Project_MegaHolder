Burger TopHat® Corporation ("BTH®," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, use our app, or interact with us at our restaurant locations in Malaysia. Please read this policy carefully.

01 — Introduction

Introduction

Burger TopHat® Corporation, together with its subsidiaries and affiliated entities ("BTH®," "we," "us," or "our"), has adopted this Privacy Policy to reflect our commitment to protecting your privacy and to inform you of our policies and practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of any Personal Information we may collect from our customers.

We take the collection of Personal Information from our customers seriously and are committed to protecting your Personal Information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable Malaysian law, including the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

BTH® only seeks to collect Personal Information as necessary to provide the products and services you request from us and as otherwise disclosed in this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy gives you clear notice of:

  • The categories of Personal Information we collect and process
  • The purposes for which we process that information
  • Whether we share your Personal Information with any third parties
  • Your rights under Malaysian law and how to exercise them

IF YOU DO NOT AGREE TO THE TERMS OF THIS PRIVACY POLICY, PLEASE DO NOT USE OUR WEBSITE, APP, OR SERVICES, AND DO NOT SUBMIT ANY PERSONAL INFORMATION THROUGH OUR PLATFORMS. BY USING OUR SERVICES, YOU ACKNOWLEDGE AND AGREE THAT WE MAY PROCESS YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION AS DESCRIBED IN THIS PRIVACY POLICY.

02 — Scope

Where This Policy Applies

This Privacy Policy applies to the following services (collectively, the "Services"):

  • Our website at www.burgertophat.com.my and any related BTH® web pages
  • The BTH® mobile ordering application (the "BTH® App")
  • In-store interactions at BTH® corporate-owned restaurants in Malaysia, where applicable laws apply
  • BTH® loyalty programme, promotions, contests, and customer satisfaction surveys
  • Our BTH® Delivery service and third-party delivery platform integrations

Please note that franchise-owned BTH® outlets may operate under their own privacy practices. We encourage you to contact those outlets directly for information about how they handle your personal data.

If a particular BTH® service links to a different privacy notice, that notice applies to that specific service. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services accessible through our platforms.

04 — Collection

Information We Collect

We collect Personal Information in the following categories:

Information You Provide Directly

  • Account & Registration: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and password when you create a BTH® account or sign up for our loyalty programme.
  • Orders & Transactions: Billing and delivery address, payment card details (processed securely via third-party payment processors), and your order history.
  • Student Verification: If you redeem a Student Price promotion, we may collect your student ID number and institution name solely to verify eligibility.
  • Communications: Any messages, feedback, or enquiries you send us via our website, app, or in-store feedback forms.
  • Promotions & Surveys: Information provided when you participate in contests, giveaways, or customer satisfaction surveys.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, and links clicked.
  • Location Data: General location data derived from your IP address, or precise location data if you enable location services on the BTH® App for the restaurant locator feature.
  • Transaction Logs: Details of orders placed through our digital platforms, including timestamps and order values.

Information from Third Parties

  • If you log in using a third-party account (e.g., Google or Facebook), we may receive basic profile information such as your name and email address from that platform, subject to your privacy settings there.
  • Delivery partners (e.g., GrabFood, FoodPanda) may share order and delivery data with us for fulfilment and customer service purposes.
05 — Technology

Information Collected Via Technology

Our website and BTH® App use automated data-collection technologies. These include:

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use the following types:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function (e.g., keeping items in your cart). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site (e.g., Google Analytics). We use this data in aggregate form only.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to show you relevant BTH® promotions on other websites. You can opt out at any time via our Cookie Preferences centre.

Web Beacons / Pixels

We may use invisible pixels in emails to track whether a message was opened and which links were clicked. This helps us improve the relevance of our communications.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings or our Cookie Preferences centre available in the footer of our website. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our Services.

06 — Use

How We Use Your Information

BTH® uses the Personal Information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Order Fulfilment: Processing, preparing, and delivering your orders; sending order confirmations and receipts.
  • Account Management: Creating and managing your BTH® account and loyalty programme membership.
  • Customer Support: Responding to your enquiries, complaints, and feedback.
  • Promotions & Marketing: Sending you news about BTH® offers, new menu items, and events via email, SMS, or push notification — only where you have given your consent or where we have a legitimate interest to do so.
  • Personalisation: Tailoring the content, offers, and recommendations you see on our platforms based on your order history and preferences.
  • Analytics & Improvement: Analysing how customers use our website, app, and restaurants so we can improve our products and services.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Complying with applicable Malaysian laws, regulations, and lawful requests from authorities.
  • Fraud Prevention & Security: Detecting, investigating, and preventing fraudulent transactions and other unlawful activity.

Opting Out of Marketing: You can unsubscribe from BTH® marketing emails at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link in any email we send, or by updating your communication preferences in your BTH® account settings.

07 — Sharing

How We Share Your Information

BTH® does not sell your Personal Information. We may share your information with the following categories of third parties only as described below:

  • Service Providers: Third-party companies that assist us in operating our Services — including payment processors, cloud hosting providers, email delivery platforms, customer support tools, and analytics providers. These parties are contractually required to use your data only on our behalf and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
  • Delivery Partners: If you place a delivery order, we share the necessary details (name, delivery address, order contents) with our delivery partners (e.g., GrabFood, FoodPanda) solely to fulfil your order.
  • Marketing Platforms: With your consent, we may share limited data with advertising platforms (e.g., Meta, Google) to show you relevant BTH® promotions. You can opt out at any time.
  • Franchise Partners: Where you interact with a franchise-operated BTH® outlet, limited transaction data may be shared with that franchisee for operational purposes.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: We may disclose your Personal Information if required to do so by law, court order, or government authority, or where we believe such disclosure is necessary to protect our legal rights or prevent harm.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our business assets, your Personal Information may be transferred to the relevant third party, subject to the same privacy protections.
08 — Retention

Data Retention

We retain your Personal Information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or as required by applicable laws and regulations.

  • Account Data: Retained for the duration of your account's active status plus 3 years after account closure, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Transaction Records: Retained for 7 years to comply with Malaysian financial and tax record-keeping requirements.
  • Marketing Preferences: Retained until you withdraw consent or request deletion.
  • Cookie & Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymised after 26 months.

When your Personal Information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise it.

09 — Security

Security

BTH® implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your Personal Information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your device and our servers
  • Payment card data processed exclusively via PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment processors — we do not store full card numbers
  • Access controls restricting employee access to Personal Information on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security assessments and monitoring of our systems

While we take reasonable steps to safeguard your Personal Information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% secure. If you suspect any unauthorised access to your BTH® account, please contact us immediately at privacy@burgertophat.com.my.

10 — Children

Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from minors. If you believe that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 18, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete such information from our records as soon as practicable.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with Personal Information without your consent, please contact us at the details in the Contact Us section below.

11 — Your Rights

Your Rights & Choices

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and other applicable Malaysian laws, you have the following rights in relation to your Personal Information:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the Personal Information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete Personal Information.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where we rely on your consent to process your Personal Information (e.g., for marketing), you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Right to Limit Processing: In certain circumstances, you may request that we restrict the processing of your Personal Information.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your Personal Information where it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to any legal retention obligations.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer using the details in the Contact Us section. We will respond to your request within 21 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

If you are dissatisfied with how we handle your Personal Information, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Department of Personal Data Protection Malaysia (JPDP).

12 — Third-Party Links

Third-Party Links

Our website and app may contain links to third-party websites or services (for example, delivery platforms, social media pages, or payment gateways). This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third-party services. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit through links on our platforms.

BTH® is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

13 — Changes

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Post a prominent notice on our website or app
  • Where required by law, notify you directly via email or in-app notification

Your continued use of our Services after the effective date of any updated Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the revised terms. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
